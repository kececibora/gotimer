⏱️ The clean, distraction-free game clock for Go — also known as Baduk (바둑) and Weiqi (围棋).

Whether it's a club game, a tournament round or training at home, Go Baduk Weiqi Timer keeps the clock fair and out of the way so you can focus on the board.

▶️ TIME SYSTEMS
• Japanese Byo-yomi — main time plus a number of fixed periods
• Canadian Byo-yomi — a fixed time for a set number of moves
• Simple / Sudden Death — a single main time

✨ FEATURES
• Large, easy-to-read tournament display
• Tap your side of the screen to pass the turn
• Per-move byo-yomi that resets correctly, just like a real clock
• Separate settings for Black and White
• Sound countdown in the final seconds
• Live settings — adjust the time without restarting
• Three themes: Classic, Wood Dojo, Cyberpunk
• Works fully offline

🌍 10 LANGUAGES
English, Türkçe, 日本語, 한국어, 简体中文, 繁體中文, Deutsch, Français, Español, Italiano.

🔒 PRIVACY
No ads. No accounts. No internet required. No personal data collected.

Place your phone between the players, start the clock, and enjoy the game.
