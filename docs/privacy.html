Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

Summary

Go Baduk Weiqi Timer does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Period.

Details

  • The app works fully offline and makes no network connections.
  • No accounts, no registration, no sign-in.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking SDKs.
  • No personal information is collected, processed, or transmitted.
  • Your timer settings are stored only on your device and never leave it.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics services.

Children

Since no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children either.
